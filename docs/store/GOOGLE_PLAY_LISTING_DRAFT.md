## App name
KickLuck

## Short description
Pick real football matches. Let luck choose the scenario.

## Full description
KickLuck is a football entertainment randomizer built for fans who want a quick, playful way to explore real upcoming fixtures.

Choose 1–5 matches, select a mode such as 1X2, Total, BTTS or Random Mix, then press Generate. Lock the picks you like and regenerate the rest.

Features:
- Real upcoming football fixtures
- 1–5 match selection
- 1X2, totals, BTTS and more
- Random Mix
- Lock and Generate Again
- Saved Picks
- History
- Shareable KickLuck cards

KickLuck does not claim to predict match outcomes or guarantee winnings. It is an entertainment randomizer.
